# Responsible Use

MasterAI is provided for research and education in game theory, imperfect-information games, reinforcement learning, search, and multi-agent decision making.

## Expected use

- Reproducible algorithm research and benchmarking
- Educational experiments with CFR and self-play
- Simulation against authorized bots in controlled environments
- Analysis of abstraction, search, value estimation, and decision latency
- Defensive study of automated decision systems

## Unacceptable use

Do not use this project to violate applicable law, platform terms, tournament rules, privacy rights, access controls, or third-party intellectual-property rights. Do not deploy it against people or services without authorization. Do not use real credentials, payment data, or private player histories in public experiments.

## Research safeguards

- Use isolated environments and synthetic or properly licensed data.
- Clearly label automated agents during authorized human-subject tests.
- Obtain appropriate consent and institutional review where required.
- Protect model, service, and data credentials.
- Publish limitations and uncertainty with performance claims.
- Review local gambling, consumer-protection, data, and AI regulations before deployment.
